What DMALL is

DMALL is a marketplace for Discord broadcasts. Buyers send a message straight to the members of a chosen server; server owners list their community as a lot and earn on every delivery. Custom emoji, stickers and attachments are all supported — with buyer protection built in: you pay per delivered message, and anything undelivered comes back.

How it works

Three steps from a server to a delivered broadcast.

1

Pick a server

Choose a community from the catalog. Every card is pre-checked so you only enter servers where broadcasts can actually deliver.

2

Compose your broadcast

Write the message, add custom emoji, stickers or media, set targeting and a schedule — send now, in a few minutes, or at a set time.

3

Launch — pay per delivered

Messages go out from a managed bot pool. You're charged per 1000 delivered; whatever doesn't land is refunded automatically.

Own a server? List it and earn

Turn your community into a lot — you set the price, you earn on every delivery.

1

Add the bot

Invite our bot to your server with admin rights — it connects your community to DMALL in a couple of minutes.

2

3

Earn on every delivery

Frequently asked

How am I charged?

Per 1000 delivered messages. You top up your wallet, launch a broadcast, and once it settles anything undelivered is refunded — you only pay for what actually landed.

What can a message contain?

Text, custom emoji and stickers from the servers our bots are on, attachments (image, video or file), and a rich embed. A live preview shows exactly how it will look.

Can I list my own server?

Yes. Add our bot as admin, create a lot and set your price per 1000 messages. You earn on every delivery other buyers make to your server.

Can I schedule or stop a broadcast?

You can send immediately, after a delay, or at a set date and time — and stop an active run at any moment. Delivery is tracked in real time.
